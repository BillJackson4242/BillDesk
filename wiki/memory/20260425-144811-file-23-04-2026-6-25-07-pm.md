# 20260425 144811 File 23 04 2026 6 25 07 Pm

## Summary
This document outlines CCCC's position on the right to refuse generative AI in writing classrooms, emphasizing concerns about linguistic homogenization and advocating for process-based pedagogy over punitive plagiarism measures.

## Key Points
- Students and teachers should have the option to avoid using generative AI.
- AI is seen as reinforcing linguistic hegemony and standard English supremacy.
- The 1974 "Students' Right to Their Own Language" resolution frames much of their argument.
- There is a concern that AI could limit rhetorical play for students by pushing towards one standardized language.
- CCCC advocates for process-based pedagogy over punitive plagiarism detection services.
